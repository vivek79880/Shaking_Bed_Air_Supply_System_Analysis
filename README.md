Objective:
This report presents an in-depth analysis of the Shaking Bed Air Supply System. The goal is to identify operational ineffi ciencies, potential risks, and optimization opportunities to enhance process performance, minimize energy consumption, and maintain product quality.

Context:
The Air Supply System is responsible for regulating key parameters such as air temperature, fan speeds, and steam valve operations. Maintaining these parameters within optimal ranges ensures effi ciency and prevents system failures.
